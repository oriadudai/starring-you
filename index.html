Good to know

Questions

Do my guests need to download anything?

No. They open the link in their phone's browser and play.

What kind of party is it for?

Birthdays, bachelorette and bachelor parties, weddings, farewells, family reunions. Anything with a guest of honor.

Can I keep it family friendly?

Yes. Family friendly is the default. You choose if you want it a little cheekier, and you approve every card.

What happens to what I write about my friend?

It is used only to make your game. Games are deleted after 90 days, and you can delete yours any time.
